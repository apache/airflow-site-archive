Durable and Deferrable Operators

252 operators, sensors and decorators across 24 providers that can reconnect to their job on retry (Durable) or wait in the Triggerer instead of holding a worker slot (Deferrable).

Durable 18
On retry, reconnects to the job it already started, or replays the progress it saved, instead of starting over. Controlled by the operator's durable flag: on by default for most, opt-in for the AI agent operators. Several only reconnect on Airflow 3.3+.
Deferrable 249
Frees the worker slot while waiting: a Triggerer does the waiting, and if it dies another Triggerer restarts the wait. Off by default: pass deferrable=True (or set [operators] default_deferrable) where the operator exposes it; some always defer.
